About DQB

Welcome to Desi Quick Bites! Located perfectly at 273 Huntington Ave, right in front of Northeastern University. We are your go-to spot for authentic, fast, and delicious Indian street food.

Whether you're craving a quick Pani Puri between classes or a comforting Biryani to end the day, our menu is crafted fresh daily with the finest ingredients to bring you the true flavors of home.
